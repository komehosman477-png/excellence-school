ADMISSIONS INFORMATION

Welcome to Excellence Admissions

We welcome applications from students who are ready to learn, grow and become part of the Excellence community.

Our admissions process is designed to make it easy for parents and guardians to submit applications and provide the necessary information about their child.

HOW TO APPLY

Admission Process

01

Submit Application

Complete the online admission application form with accurate student and guardian information.

02

Application Review

The school reviews the submitted application and contacts the parent or guardian when necessary.

03

Assessment

Depending on the class, the student may be required to participate in an assessment or interview.

04

Admission Decision

Successful applicants receive information about admission and the next steps.

WHAT YOU NEED

Admission Requirements

Junior Secondary School

  • Completed application form
  • Previous school records
  • Birth certificate or valid identification
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Parent/Guardian information

Senior Secondary School

  • Completed application form
  • Previous academic records
  • Birth certificate or valid identification
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Parent/Guardian information
